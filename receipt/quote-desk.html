The price is parameter-aware: a 10 SOL probe is quoted 4405 atomic, the 1 SOL request actually paid is 3007. The authoritative figure is always the maxAmountRequired in that request's own 402 (3007 for the call shown here).

2   The signed payment: X-PAYMENT

An EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization for the Base USDC contract, signed by the payer with a zero-gas EIP-712 signature, then base64-encoded into the X-PAYMENT header and the request retried. The 65-byte signature is elided; the authorization fields, including the live nonce, are the ones that settled.

{
  "x402Version": 1,
  "scheme": "exact",
  "network": "base",
  "payload": {
    "signature": "0x<65-byte ECDSA signature over the EIP-712 digest>",
    "authorization": {
      "from": "0x7373e88368378052eb5a449d2ebc95ad7e5f2bef",
      "to": "0xC22f661733B85C1349F7C83EE0B2BCF3234603dE",
      "value": "3007",
      "validAfter": "<block.timestamp - 600>",
      "validBefore": "<block.timestamp + 60>",
