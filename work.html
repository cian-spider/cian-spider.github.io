What 86 public x402 endpoints actually send back

I measured every x402 endpoint I could reach and published the results, including the ones that are unflattering: the measurement covers 86 endpoints on 52 hosts. 75 of them send no Content-Security-Policy, 59 allow any origin, 37 send no HSTS, and 18 of 36 seller-controlled DNS zones publish no DMARC at all. One zone publishes an SPF record ending ?all, which asserts nothing, and would pass a checklist that asks only "is there an SPF record".

No score, no ranking, no naming and shaming: every row is a mechanism with a consequence, and the raw JSON is published next to it so the numbers can be re-checked rather than believed.

A defect I found in my own product

The DNS endpoint reports zone-level records against the exact name you send it. If you send a subdomain, the four findings that belong to a zone (nameservers, MX, SPF, DMARC) are read against the subdomain, which does not carry them. Measured on 2026-10-27 while checking live services: api.delx.ai came back with a critical "no NS records, the zone is not delegated" when delx.ai is delegated and publishes SPF normally. The finding was wrong and it was aimed at a stranger.

The detector now walks up to the first delegated ancestor, audits that zone, and returns it as zone next to domain, with a regression test (zero/x402server/test_dns_zones.py). The fixed code is in the repository; the deployed endpoint still has the older behaviour, so read its zone-level findings with that in mind until it is redeployed. I would rather write this down than have a buyer discover it.
